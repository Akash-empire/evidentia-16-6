Evaluation • Nutrition Tamil Nadu Government

Tamil Nadu Breakfast Scheme

Evaluated implementation to understand how a large-scale school nutrition programme reaches children on the ground.

Evidentia's Role: Conducted a mixed-method evaluation examining programme implementation, beneficiary coverage, meal consumption, school-level processes and nutritional aspects.
What We Did
  • Survey-based data collection
  • Focus Group Discussions & In-Depth Interviews
  • Direct observation of school kitchens & implementation
  • Assessment of food safety & meal delivery
  • Quantitative & qualitative analysis
Evidence Examined
  • School attendance & meal consumption
  • Student activity & peer interactions
  • Implementation processes & food safety
  • Nutritional adequacy
Contribution & Impact

The Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu C. Joseph Vijay has implemented the evaluation's recommendation provided by Evidentia as third party evaluator. Recommendation suggested to scale for middle school until 8th grade and increasing the supply to more govt and govt aided schools.

Needs Assessment • Inclusion Dept. of Social Welfare, TN

Communication Needs Assessment for Persons with Disabilities

Understanding how persons with disabilities and caregivers access, understand and use information about government welfare schemes.

Evidentia's Role: Conducted a mixed-methods assessment across five districts of Tamil Nadu to identify communication barriers, information preferences and gaps in access.
What We Did
  • 301 respondent surveys across 5 districts
  • 5 Focus Group Discussions & 25 In-Depth Interviews
  • Inclusive & accessible research tools
  • Descriptive statistical & thematic narrative analysis
What We Explored
  • Awareness of disability certification & early intervention
  • Scholarships, loans & travel concessions
  • Assistive-device schemes & access channels
Key Insights & Contribution

Identified barriers in digital access, literacy, and physical formats. Preferred channels included WhatsApp, social media, TV/YouTube and word of mouth.

Contribution: Supported evidence-based recommendations for accessible government communication.

Nutrition • Public Health Sapiens Health Foundation

SAPIENS: Capacity Building for Salt Reduction

Translating scientific evidence on dietary salt reduction into practical guidance for healthcare practitioners.

Evidentia's Role: Developed a capacity-building manual for healthcare practitioners on salt reduction, covering scientific evidence, public health strategies, clinical considerations, and practical approaches to patient counselling.
WHAT THE MANUAL COVERED
  • NCDs & Modifiable Risk Factors: Non-communicable diseases, dietary patterns, unhealthy food consumption, and healthier lifestyle practices.
  • Salt, Sodium & Health: Dietary salt recommendations, salt consumption in India, health impacts, myths about salt, and sodium–potassium balance.
  • Salt-Reduction Strategies: Public health approaches, initiatives in India, and practical strategies for reducing salt intake through dietary choices.
  • Low-Sodium Salt Substitutes: Potential benefits, potassium intake, availability and regulation in India, and clinical considerations for use and monitoring.
  • Patient Counselling & Dietary Guidance: Step-by-step counselling guidance, key messages, practical recommendations for reducing salt intake, low-salt recipes, and sample diet plans based on Indian dietary patterns.
PROJECT CONTEXT

Sapiens Health Foundation, in partnership with Resolve to Save Lives and public health departments, supported capacity building for healthcare professionals on low-sodium salt substitutes and patient counselling. The wider initiative trained more than 300 physicians across India through workshops held in Chennai, Mumbai, and Delhi.

EVIDENTIA'S CONTRIBUTION

Evidentia Research Solutions developed the Manual on Capacity Building for Health Care Practitioners on Salt Reduction. The manual brought together scientific information, public health strategies, clinical guidance, and practical dietary recommendations to support healthcare practitioners in promoting dietary salt reduction and counselling patients.

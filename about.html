Where It All Began

At the heart of Evidentia is Dr. K. Niraimathi Anandan, whose career has been shaped by over 25 years of experience in public health nutrition, community-based research, programme evaluation, and capacity building. She has worked closely with government bodies, NGOs, academic institutions, and CSR initiatives to address complex social and public health challenges.


Her vision for Evidentia was never to be just another research organization, but a trusted partner that helps organizations ask the right questions, generate credible evidence, and make informed decisions. Today, that philosophy continues to shape every project, partnership, and solution delivered by the team.

Why Choose us ?

STEP 01

Research with Purpose

Every project begins with understanding the real problem—not simply producing another report. We work closely with clients to identify objectives, define research questions, and create evidence-driven strategies that lead to meaningful outcomes and long-term impact.

STEP 02

End-to-End Expertise

From research design and field implementation to data analysis, reporting, and capacity building, we support every stage of the research journey. Our integrated approach ensures consistency, quality, and actionable outcomes from start to finish.

STEP 03

Methodological Excellence

Our multidisciplinary team combines rigorous academic knowledge with extensive practical research experience. We apply proven methodologies, robust analytical techniques, and internationally recognised standards to deliver reliable and trustworthy findings.

STEP 04

Evidence for Action

We believe research should lead to action. Beyond collecting and analysing data, we help organisations transform evidence into informed decisions, effective policies, strategic planning, and measurable social impact that creates lasting value.

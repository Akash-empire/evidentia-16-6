Meet Evidentia

Evidentia Research Solutions helps turn complex questions into meaningful evidence. We partner with researchers, organizations, NGOs, businesses, and governments to deliver end-to-end research that informs decisions and creates lasting impact.

"Research is meaningful when it helps someone make a better decision.”

At Evidentia, we believe research should guide future actions. Every project here is driven by scientific rigour, ethical practice, and a commitment to creating evidence and delivers meaningful outcome.
